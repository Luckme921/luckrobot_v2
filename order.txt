==========================================
推荐的一键入口（2026-08 导航可靠性升级）
==========================================
开荒/继续建图：
ros2 launch my_robot_bringup smart_navigation.launch.py mode:=mapping

自动探索（另开终端）：
ros2 launch my_robot_mapping auto_explore.launch.py

同时保存 posegraph 与栅格地图：
mkdir -p ~/ros2_ws/maps
ros2 service call /slam_toolbox/serialize_map slam_toolbox/srv/SerializePoseGraph "{filename: '/home/nvidia/ros2_ws/maps/my_room_map'}"
ros2 run nav2_map_server map_saver_cli -f /home/nvidia/ros2_ws/maps/my_room_map

日常 posegraph 定位导航（不启动 AMCL）：
ros2 launch my_robot_bringup smart_navigation.launch.py mode:=localization posegraph:=/home/nvidia/ros2_ws/maps/my_room_map

加载旧 posegraph 并永久更新地图：
ros2 launch my_robot_bringup smart_navigation.launch.py mode:=lifelong posegraph:=/home/nvidia/ros2_ws/maps/my_room_map
